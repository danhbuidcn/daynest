Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-15 · Applies to the Daynest Android app (com.danhbuidcn.daynest).

Daynest is a personal calendar, notes, to-do and expense app. This page explains what data the app handles and why. The app is for people aged 18 and over.

Data we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Content you create: calendar events, notes, to-dos, expenses and categories, budgets, recurring expenses, menstrual-cycle days (only if you turn that feature on), and your settings (time zone, theme, reminders).
  • Bug reports you send: your message, up to 3 screenshots you attach, and the app version, device model, Android version and language.
  • Devices: a device name and an internal session id, so that you can stay signed in on a limited number of devices.
  • Purchases and ads (from the version that adds them): purchase status of Daynest Pro, handled by Google Play and RevenueCat; the advertising ID and similar data used by Google AdMob to show ads. Users in the EU/UK are asked for consent before ads are personalised.

How we use it

  • To show your content on all your devices and keep it in sync.
  • To send reminders you set (scheduled on your device).
  • To fix problems you report.
  • To show ads and unlock Pro (which removes ads).

We do not sell your data. We do not use your content for advertising.

Google Calendar

If you turn on Google Calendar sync, the app asks for access to your calendar events (scope calendar.events). Access tokens are stored only on your device. Events are read from and written to your Google Calendar to keep both in sync, and are copied to your Daynest account as your events. You can turn sync off in Settings, or remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Daynest's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored, and who processes it

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage): stores your account and content.
  • Google (sign-in, Calendar, Play, AdMob) and RevenueCat (purchase status).

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each user can read only their own data.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all its data inside the app: Settings › Account › Delete account. If you can no longer use the app, follow the steps on the delete-account page. Subscriptions are managed by Google Play and are not cancelled automatically; cancel them in Google Play.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any of your content in the app.
  • Turn off notifications, Google sync and the cycle feature in Settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children

Daynest is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above. Material changes are also announced in the app.
